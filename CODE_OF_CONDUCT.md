# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, assume good faith, give actionable feedback, and focus on the portability problem and its evidence. Do not publish private data, secrets, or unverified claims.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the project maintainers. Maintainers may remove or reject contributions that violate this code.
